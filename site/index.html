How We Work

A proposed workflow that can be tailored to Melbourne manufacturing schedules, location access and vehicle logistics.

  1. 01

    Discovery & Brief

    Tell us which models you need to show, who the work is for and where it will be used.

  2. 02

    Shot List & Prep

    We agree on the shot list, location and styling before the shoot.

  3. 03

    Production Day

    We light and photograph the vehicle and film the agreed shots on location.

  4. 04

    Edit & Grade

    We retouch stills, edit the films and grade colour to keep the campaign consistent.

  5. 05

    Channel Handoff

    Receive files for the agreed channels and confirm how they can be used.
